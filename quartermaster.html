BUILD NOTES

The Philosophy

01

Prepare First

The Quartermaster approaches combat like an inventory problem. Know what is available, understand the battlefield, and choose the correct tool before committing resources.

02

Strike With Purpose

Spellstrike turns a carefully selected spell into a weapon. The goal isn't to attack more. It's to make the right attack matter.

03

Stay Mobile

The Laughing Shadow study rewards positioning, mobility, and finding opportunities to attack from unexpected angles.

04

Keep Options Open

A Magus doesn't need to solve every problem with a sword. When the battlefield changes, the Quartermaster changes with it.

BATTLEFIELD DOCTRINE

Combat Tactics

01 Opening the Engagement

Establish positioning before committing major resources. Look for isolated targets, useful terrain, and opportunities to approach from an unexpected angle.

02 The Spellstrike

When a high-value target presents itself, combine the right spell with a weapon strike and turn one action into a meaningful tactical investment.

03 Disengage and Reposition

The Quartermaster isn't interested in standing in the same square simply because he started there. Movement is part of the strategy.

04 Preserve Resources

Not every enemy deserves the full inventory. Save the strongest tools for encounters where they will have the greatest impact.
